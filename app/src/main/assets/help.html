Welcome to AI Journal Companion. Write about your day and the app tells you the main emotion in your writing, plus one line of supportive advice.

Getting around

  • ← Back (top left) returns to the previous screen. The phone's Back button does the same.
  • Home (top right) jumps straight to the main menu.
  • ⓘ Info (top right) opens this Help guide from any screen.

1. Write a journal entry

  1. On the main menu, tap New Journal Entry.
  2. Type how you feel in the text box.
  3. Tap Analyze Emotion, or press Enter/Done on the keyboard.
  4. Your result shows the emotion with an emoji and a piece of advice.
  5. Tap Clear to empty the box, New Entry to start again, or History to see past entries.

Possible emotions: JOY 😊 SADNESS 😢 ANGER 😡 FEAR 😨 SURPRISE 😲

2. Search your history

  1. Open View History.
  2. Type an emotion (for example joy) or tap one of the emotion chips.
  3. Choose the search method from the Search with dropdown: HashMap, Binary Tree or Doubly Linked List.
  4. The number of matches and the search time appear under the buttons.

3. Sort by emotion

Use the Sort by emotion dropdown and pick Bubble, Insertion or Selection Sort. Entries are ordered A–Z by emotion (newest first within the same emotion) and the time taken is shown.

4. Delete an entry (drag and drop)

  1. Press and hold an entry until it shrinks slightly.
  2. Drag it down onto the red Drop here to delete zone.
  3. When the zone says Release to delete, let go.

Dropping anywhere else puts the entry back.

5. See your emotion chart

Tap Show Chart on the History screen for a pie chart popup, or open Emotion Insights from the main menu for the full-screen chart and legend.

FAQ

"Couldn't reach the AI"?
The backend must be running on your computer. Start Ollama, then run start_backend.bat in the backend folder, and try again.

The first analysis is slow.
The AI model loads into memory the first time. Later requests are faster.

Are my entries saved?
Entries are kept in memory while the app is open and are cleared when the app closes.
